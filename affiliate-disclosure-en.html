01

Commercial relationship

Some links for flights, hotels, transport, car rental, experiences and travel insurance are affiliate links. If a user completes a booking or purchase that the seller recognizes as valid, AUTUMN KYOTO may receive a commission.

There is no extra cost to the user.

Affiliate commission is settled between the seller and this site's operator.

02

An independently edited travel guide

AUTUMN KYOTO is not an official website, branch, agency or customer-service channel of an external seller. We do not make contracts, take payment or process refunds for sellers.

03

Affiliate links and tracking

Links may contain identifiers used by the seller's affiliate system to connect clicks with valid bookings. We do not use deceptive shortened destinations or encourage invalid clicks or bookings.

04

Prices, availability and terms

Prices, taxes, exchange rates, inventory, operating dates and cancellation terms can change. Always use the seller's final checkout screen as the source of truth.

05

Bookings, changes and refunds

The booking contract is between the user and the external seller or service provider. Contact the seller for confirmation, payment errors, changes, cancellation, refunds or on-site service issues.

06

Images and editorial content

Atmosphere images do not guarantee the condition of a room, train or experience. Check the seller's current photos and product details before choosing.

07

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when sellers, link structures or applicable standards change.
