03 / KYOTO TRAVEL PREP

Six things to check
before you fly.

Kyoto's autumn days can feel much warmer than mornings and evenings. Recheck the forecast just before departure and use light layers to keep luggage manageable.

01 / WHAT TO WEAR

Clothes by month

Consider rain, wind, shade and evening illuminations—not only the headline temperature.

SEPEARLY AUTUMN

T-shirt + light layer

Days can still be hot and humid. Bring breathable tops and a thin shirt or windbreaker.

  • Compact umbrella
  • Quick-dry socks
  • Sun hat
OCTMID AUTUMN

Long sleeves + layers

Dress lightly while walking, then add a cardigan or light jacket in shaded temples and after sunset.

  • Long-sleeve base
  • Cardigan or light jacket
  • Cushioned walking shoes
NOVPEAK FOLIAGE

Knitwear + warm jacket

Early mornings and evening illuminations can feel cold. Add compact warmth such as a scarf or thin thermal layer.

  • Warm outer layer
  • Scarf and light gloves
  • Warm socks

Weather varies each year. Make the final adjustment using Kyoto's hourly forecast about three days before departure.

04 / PASSPORT & ENTRYCheck your passport
twice before departure.

Passport and entry

01
Name and passport validity

Your ticket name must match your passport. Check both Japan's requirements and any additional airline validity rules.

02
Visa rules for your nationality

Visa-free conditions vary by nationality, purpose and length of stay. Confirm with Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

03
Immigration and customs

Check whether to use Visit Japan Web and save essential QR screens in case mobile data is unavailable.

05 / TRAVEL NOTES

Useful notes for
travelling in Japan.

Rules vary by venue. Follow posted signs and staff guidance first.

歩

Walking and transport

Traffic keeps left. Watch for bicycles, and keep calls and loud conversations off trains.

寺

Temples and photos

Respect no-photo areas, tripod limits and closing times. Do not block narrow residential lanes.

薬

Bringing medicines

Ingredients and quantities may be restricted. Check official rules and carry prescriptions where needed.

税

Payment and tax-free shopping

Your original passport may be required. Check the final price, exchange rate and card fees.

荷

Luggage and crowds

Large cases are difficult on buses. Consider hotel storage, delivery services or rail travel.

緊

Emergencies

Police: 110. Ambulance and fire: 119. For a lost passport, report it and contact your embassy.
